Color Harmony Rules

Monochromatic

Variations in lightness and saturation of a single hue. Creates a cohesive, refined look.

Complementary

Colors opposite on the color wheel. High contrast and vibrant energy.

Analogous

Adjacent colors on the wheel (3-4 hues). Smooth, harmonious feel.

Triadic

Three evenly spaced colors (120° apart). Balanced vibrancy.

Tetradic

Two complementary pairs. The richest color combinations.
